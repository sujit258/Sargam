Sargam was inspired by and developed from the open-source architecture of **Mehfil**, originally created by Shashwat Tripathi ([github.com/shashwa7-dev/mehfil](https://github.com/shashwa7-dev/mehfil)).

Sargam is an independent product developed and maintained by our engineering team, featuring an original visual identity, multi-language architecture, expanded discovery modes, the Sargam Catalog Engine, and regional catalog support (starting with Marathi).

## 3. Audio & Music Catalogue Disclaimer

- **Zero Hosted Audio:** No audio files, MP3s, or recordings are hosted, stored, uploaded, or served from Sargam servers.
- **Embedded Playback:** Every song is played directly through YouTube's official embedded player (`www.youtube-nocookie.com`) from uploads published by official record labels and rights holders. Playback metrics, view counts, and monetization remain entirely with YouTube and the respective rights owners.
- **Factual Song Metadata:** Song titles, film names, composers, lyricists, and singers are factual historical catalog data compiled from publicly distributed songlists. Recordings, compositions, and lyrics remain the intellectual property of their respective copyright holders.
