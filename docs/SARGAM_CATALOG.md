# SARGAM Music Catalogue Architecture

## 1. Overview
The Sargam Music Catalogue represents the cultural core of the product. The foundation release (`v1.0.0`) contains **3,916 verified songs across 66 stations**, representing 415 artists, 1,379 films, 23 composers, and 12 lyricists.

---

## 2. Station Taxonomy

The 66 stations are organized into thematic listening dials:

1. **Iconic Vocalists (Singers):**
   Lata Mangeshkar, Asha Bhosle, Kishore Kumar, Mohammed Rafi, Mukesh, Manna Dey, Geeta Dutt, Hemant Kumar, Talat Mahmood, Jagjit Singh, Yesudas, Shamshad Begum & Noor Jehan, Suraiya & Rajkumari, Mangeshkars.
2. **Visionary Composers:**
   R.D. Burman, S.D. Burman, Shankar-Jaikishan, Laxmikant-Pyarelal, Kalyanji-Anandji, Naushad, O.P. Nayyar, Madan Mohan, Salil Chowdhury, Anil Biswas, Khayyam, Bappi Lahiri, Rajesh Roshan, Roshans, C. Ramchandra Chitragupta, Anand-Milind Jatin-Lalit.
3. **Poets & Lyricists:**
   Anand Bakshi, Gulzar, Sahir Ludhianvi, Majrooh Sultanpuri, Shailendra, Hasrat Jaipuri, Shakeel Badayuni, Rajendra Krishan, Kaifi Azmi, Javed Akhtar, Indeevar, Bharat Vyas.
4. **Cinematic Stars (Actors):**
   Amitabh Bachchan, Bachchans, Rekha, Hema Malini, Rishi Kapoor, Shashi Kapoor, Kapoors, Amol Palekar & Sanjeev Kumar, Smita Patil & Shabana Azmi, Zeenat Aman & Parveen Babi, Sadhana & Babita.
5. **Moods & Formats:**
   Happy, Sad, Romance, Bhakti, Sufi, Ghazal, Gurbani, Film Instrumental, Hindustani Classical, Duet Hits, Songs With Dialogues, Top 300.
