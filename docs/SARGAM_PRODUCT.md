# SARGAM — Product Specification & Vision

## 1. Product Summary
**Sargam — Retro Bollywood Melodies** ([https://sargam.suvidhatools.in](https://sargam.suvidhatools.in)) is an independent, production-grade Progressive Web Application (PWA) engineered for exploring and enjoying golden-era Indian film music (1950s–1980s) and regional masterworks (commencing with Marathi).

---

## 2. Target Audience & Positioning
- **Audience:** Connoisseurs of golden-era Indian cinema, diaspora listeners seeking authentic vintage recordings, lovers of Marathi Bhavgeet and Natya Sangeet, and music enthusiasts seeking an ad-free, account-free listening sanctum.
- **Atmosphere:** Classic Bollywood and regional music playing in an intimate vintage listening room illuminated by warm golden studio light.
- **Values:** Respect for original creators, zero telemetry profiling, zero music hosting, high-performance offline capability, and accessible typography.

---

## 3. Core Features
1. **Curated Station Dials:** 66 stations categorizing 3,916 songs across iconic vocalists (Lata Mangeshkar, Kishore Kumar, Mohammed Rafi, Mukesh, Asha Bhosle), composers (R.D. Burman, S.D. Burman, Naushad, Khayyam), lyricists (Sahir Ludhianvi, Gulzar, Anand Bakshi), and cinema genres.
2. **Aajcha Sargam (आजचा सरगम):** Deterministic daily song of the day synchronized identically for all listeners across the globe without requiring server storage.
3. **Multi-Language Architecture:** First-class regional expansion starting with Marathi (Bhavgeet, Natya Sangeet, Golden Cinema, Bhakti).
4. **Golden Eras Navigation:** Dedicated portals into the 1950s (Black & White Melody), 1960s (Golden Dawn), 1970s (Technicolor Harmony), and 1980s (Retro Rhythm).
5. **Surprise Me Engine:** Intelligent weighted shuffle discovering underplayed gems.
6. **Local Privacy:** Favorites and playback history stored strictly on-device in `localStorage`.
